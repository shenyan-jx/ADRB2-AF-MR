v1.0.0 — analysis code and result tables

Replication materials for: Sun G, Shen Y. The β2-adrenoceptor as a causal pathway
to atrial fibrillation: a drug-target Mendelian randomization study informing
long-acting β2-agonist prescribing in COPD (in preparation for the European Journal of Clinical Pharmacology).

Contents
- R_scripts/        14 R scripts (ADRB2 and ADRB1 pipelines, sensitivity analyses, figures, audit)
- result_tables/    10 CSV files (mr_af/ and mr_adrb1/)
- README.md, LICENSE, CITATION.cff, .zenodo.json

Data: eQTLGen eqtl-a-ENSG00000169252; FinnGen finn-b-I9_AF; retrieved 12 November 2026.
Software: R 4.6.1; TwoSampleMR 0.7.13; ieugwasr; ggplot2.

This Release is archived by Zenodo with a version DOI, which is cited in the manuscript.
